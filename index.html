Our Core Values

🙏 Prayer

We believe in the power of prayer and encourage believers to maintain a consistent prayer life with God.

❤️ Love & Unity

We promote love, unity, and togetherness among believers as one family in Christ.

📖 Faith in God's Word

We stand on the teachings of Jesus Christ and the truth of God's Word as our spiritual foundation.

🌍 Evangelism

We are committed to sharing the Gospel and reaching others with the message of Christ.

🤝 Fellowship & Support

We encourage believers through fellowship, care, and practical support for one another.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To build believers through prayer, fellowship, evangelism and support for one another.

Our Vision

To become a prayer-driven family where lives are transformed through faith and the power of God.
