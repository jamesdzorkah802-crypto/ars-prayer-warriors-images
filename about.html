Our History

ARS Prayer Warriors is a prayer ministry of the Apostles Revelation Society (ARS), founded by ADMIN KING JAMES DZORKA on 30th September 2021.

The ministry was established under the spiritual foundation laid by PROPHET C.K.N. WOVENU, the Founder of the Apostles Revelation Society, whose vision emphasizes prayer, holiness, evangelism, and steadfast faith in Jesus Christ.

Through the Church's Prayer Union, members were encouraged to develop a consistent prayer life and remain spiritually committed.

As the Church continued to grow, many members relocated from their local branches to different towns, cities, and regions where there were no branches of the Apostles Revelation Society. Many found it difficult to maintain regular fellowship, participate in activities, receive information, and remain spiritually encouraged.

Recognizing this growing need, ADMIN KING JAMES DZORKA, through prayer and God's guidance, established ARS PRAYER WARRIORS as a ministry under the Apostles Revelation Society.

The ministry was created to provide a united prayer platform that keeps believers spiritually connected regardless of their geographical location.

ARS Prayer Warriors exists to strengthen believers through prayer, promote Christian fellowship, encourage evangelism, provide practical support, and contribute to the spiritual and numerical growth of the Apostles Revelation Society.

Our Commitment to the Apostles Revelation Society (ARS)

ARS Prayer Warriors is an official ministry operating under the Apostles Revelation Society (ARS).

We remain fully committed to the vision, doctrines, constitution, teachings, and spiritual leadership of the Church.

We do not exist to create division, establish a separate church, or promote teachings that conflict with the doctrines and principles of the Apostles Revelation Society.

Rather, we exist to complement the mission of the Church by strengthening prayer lives, promoting Christian fellowship, encouraging evangelism, and providing practical support to members.
